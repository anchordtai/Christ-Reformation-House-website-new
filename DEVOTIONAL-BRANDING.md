# UNLEASH THE SUPERNATURAL

Permanent series title: **UNLEASH THE SUPERNATURAL**.

Tagline: **Daily encounters with God’s Word, power and purpose.**

The overarching name precedes each day's individual topic in the public reader and the editor's reading preview. The reader hero, page titles, social captions and downloaded memory-verse cards share that identity. All existing monthly themes, individual daily topics, manuscript text and release times are retained.

Artwork is stored in `public/assets/img/unleash-the-supernatural-banner.webp`. It was created with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP for the site; title and publisher text remain accessible HTML rather than being baked into the photo. The original generation is retained separately.

Final artwork prompt: premium panoramic Christian devotional banner; a realistic open Bible on the right, warm golden light through deep emerald and teal clouds, dignified and hopeful editorial style, dark uncluttered space on the left for live website typography. No people, faces, hands, lettering, logo or watermark.
